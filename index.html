Our story

We met on a rainy Tuesday in a coffee line that wasn't moving fast enough for either of us. Four years, two apartments, and one very stubborn cat later, Luis asked Sara to marry him on the same trail where they had their first real conversation.

We can't wait to marry each other surrounded by the people who've shaped our story so far — that's you.
